New posts go up on Thursdays, and you don't have to remember to check back.
Pick whichever of these suits you.

## By email

{{< subscribe-form >}}

You get one email per new post: its title, a short description, and a link to
read it here. The first email asks you to confirm, and every email has a link
to unsubscribe. The emails are sent through
[Buttondown](https://buttondown.com), a small newsletter service.

## In a feed reader

A feed reader is an app that checks the sites you follow and gathers their new
posts in one place, like an inbox you control. Paste this address into one:

`https://londopy.github.io/blog/index.xml`

Or use the [JSON Feed](https://londopy.github.io/blog/api/v1/feed.json), if
your reader prefers it. There are free readers for every platform, for example
[NetNewsWire](https://netnewswire.com/) on iPhone and Mac,
[Feeder](https://f-droid.org/packages/com.nononsenseapps.feeder/) on Android,
and [Inoreader](https://www.inoreader.com/) or [Feedly](https://feedly.com/) on
the web.
