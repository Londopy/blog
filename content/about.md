I'm Londopy. I'm studying engineering with a focus on field work, and heading toward OT security: the computers that run power grids, water plants, and factories. Most of what I build starts as a question I can't stop turning over.

Away from the keyboard, I surf, climb, and freedive. I've been pescatarian for more than eight years, and Daoist philosophy shapes how I think about most things.

## What I write about

Mostly security and systems: git internals, tooling, and the industrial control systems I'm heading toward. Some radio. And now and then, whatever I've been reading or eating, like the history of which animals religions have counted as fish.

## How this blog works

New posts go up on Thursdays. Everything publishes here first, and copies on other sites link back to the original. If you spot a mistake, tell me through the [contact page]({{< relref "contact" >}}) and I'll fix it.
